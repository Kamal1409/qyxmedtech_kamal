Sperm analysis software · accuracy update · 30 Sep 2026

Our results are now closer to the LensHooke lab analyzer

We compared our software with the lab report on 21 semen samples. After the update it is closer to the lab on 6 of 7 measurements: sperm count moved from 38.8 to 7.5 million/mL off, swimming speed from 12.3 to 2.5 µm/s off. It got slightly worse on moving in place.

What we changed

  • Speed: the lab analyzer records video slower than it plays it back, so our software was reading every sperm as about 1.6 times too fast. We now use the real recording speed.
  • Following each sperm: in crowded samples the software sometimes mixed up a still sperm with a neighbour swimming past, which made still sperm look like they were moving. It now only links a sperm to positions it could physically reach between frames.
  • Round cells: some round cells and debris were being counted as sperm that do not move. The software now recognises them by their round shape and leaves them out.
  • Sperm moving in place: sperm that wiggle without getting anywhere were counted as not moving. They are now counted as moving.
  • Sperm count: our count now includes a correction for the analyzer's counting chamber, measured against the lab reports.
  • We adjusted these settings on 14 samples and then checked them on 7 other samples that were kept aside.

What this does not show yet

  • This is 21 samples. Before clinical use we need a larger set and a second lab's reports.
  • The lab analyzer itself varies by about 8–10% between repeat runs of the same sample, so small differences are within its own noise.
  • "Moving in place" is still unreliable and is not ready to report on its own.
  • Two settings were estimated from our samples because the analyzer's maker does not publish them (its camera speed and counting chamber). Replacing them with the maker's official values is the next step.
