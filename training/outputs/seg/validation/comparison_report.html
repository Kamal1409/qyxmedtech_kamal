CASA pipeline: before vs after

Split: all · 21 videos with lab reports · generated 2026-09-30 20:15 · Before vs After
Summary. Error (MAE) went down on 13 of 15 metrics and up on 2 (Non-progressive (NP), ALH).
What changed
  • Before = old detector (Aug 29 weights) with the previous settings; After = same detector with the changes below. Videos 18 and 23 are excluded from both.
  • Velocities: the LensHooke's exported videos play at 30 fps but it captures at ~19 fps, so every per-second value (VCL/VSL/VAP/BCF) now uses capture_fps: 19 (fitted on dev videos; replace with the spec value if Bonraybio provides it).
  • Tracking: search radius 30 -> 20 px. A fast sperm moves at most ~8 px per frame here; the wider radius let the tracker hand a still sperm's track to a passing neighbour in crowded fields, which read as false motility.
  • Round cells: a static object whose blob is round (long/short axis <= 1.2) is dropped from motility and concentration; moving objects are never dropped.
  • Non-progressive: a head that makes no progress (VAP < 3 um/s) but jiggles in place (VCL >= 6 um/s) is NP instead of immotile.
  • Concentration: x1.583 calibration for the cassette's unpublished counting volume (median lab/predicted ratio on dev videos).
  • All settings were tuned on the 14 dev videos only (leave-one-out checked); the 7 test videos (9, 11, 13, 14, 15, 21, 22) were scored once per change, after tuning.
  • Tried and rejected on dev: tail-based round-cell and NP rules (tail masks attach to nearly every head), other smoothing windows, weighting tracks by duration, a median-step motility check, reliability-weighted tuning, and a 10 px search radius.

How each number is calculated

Error
predicted − lab value, per video (lab = LensHooke report).
MAE
mean of |error| over the videos — the typical size of a miss, in the metric's own unit. Lower is better.
Bias
mean of the signed error — whether the pipeline systematically reads high (+) or low (−). Closer to 0 is better.
Pearson r
how well predictions rise and fall with the lab values (ranking), ignoring any constant offset.
ICC(2,1)
agreement: like r but also penalises offsets and scale errors. >0.9 excellent, 0.75–0.9 good, <0.5 poor.
Within ±5 pp
videos whose motility category is within 5 percentage points of the lab.
